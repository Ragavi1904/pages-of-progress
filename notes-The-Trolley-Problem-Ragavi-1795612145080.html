The Trolley Problem

Ragavi • CSE-2 • 11/25/2026
4:38
The Dilemma: Pull a lever to divert a runaway trolley from 5 workers onto 1 worker, or do nothing. Utilitarianism: Pull—saving 5 lives outweighs losing 1. Deontology: Don't pull—actively killing 1 innocent person is inherently wrong. Key Insight: Our moral intuition shifts when harm feels direct and personal (like pushing someone off a bridge) versus indirect (pulling a lever), even with the exact same outcome.
